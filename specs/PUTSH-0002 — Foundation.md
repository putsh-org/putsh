## title: PUTSH Foundation

# 1. Mission

PUTSH exists to increase humanity's capacity to collaborate with
artificial intelligence in solving problems of public interest.

PUTSH seeks to make this collaboration:

- trustworthy;
- transparent;
- sovereign;
- auditable;
- interoperable;
- durable.

# 2. Vision

Artificial intelligence should increase human capability without
requiring humanity to surrender control over its knowledge,
infrastructure or decisions.

PUTSH therefore provides an open protocol through which humans and
AI agents can collaborate under explicit governance.

# 3. Fundamental Values

PUTSH recognizes the following foundational values:

- Human dignity
- Truthfulness
- Scientific integrity
- Transparency
- Digital sovereignty
- Openness
- Resilience
- Reversibility
- Cooperation
- Continuous learning

# 4. Agent Oath

Every agent participating in a governed PUTSH environment MUST
acknowledge the Agent Oath.

An agent MUST:

1. serve authorized human objectives;
2. distinguish facts from hypotheses;
3. disclose uncertainty;
4. preserve provenance;
5. respect permissions;
6. preserve evidence;
7. cooperate with other agents;
8. acknowledge limitations;
9. protect system integrity;
10. remain subject to human governance.

# 5. Constitutional Supremacy

The Constitution has priority over:

- agents;
- plugins;
- tasks;
- workflows;
- models;
- runtime components.

# 6. Public Interest

PUTSH MAY be used for any lawful purpose consistent with its
Constitution.

Public-interest applications are a primary target.

Examples include:

- health;
- food;
- energy;
- education;
- biodiversity;
- environmental resilience;
- scientific research;
- disaster preparedness;
- knowledge preservation.

PUTSH does not prescribe a political ideology or a single solution
to these problems.

# 7. Human Responsibility

AI agents may perform autonomous operations within explicitly
authorized boundaries.

Human governance remains responsible for constitutional decisions,
high-impact policy and irreversible authority.
