# Our Mission

PUTSH (Useful and Technological Products in the Service of Humanity) is an open protocol for collaboration between human beings and artificial intelligences.

Its mission is to enable the creation, validation, transmission, and improvement of useful knowledge in order to solve problems of general interest.

PUTSH is not an artificial intelligence.

PUTSH is not a model provider.

PUTSH is a trust infrastructure that enables multiple artificial intelligences, humans, and organizations to work together according to common rules.

---

# Our Vision

We believe that artificial intelligences will become essential tools for civilization.

However, their value will not depend solely on their computing power.

It will depend on their ability to:

- collaborate;
- explain their decisions;
- follow common rules;
- protect knowledge;
- preserve user sovereignty.

PUTSH aims to become this common layer.

An open layer.

Portable.

Verifiable.

Durable.

Independent of providers.

---

# Our Objectives

Build a global library of useful knowledge.

Create a common language between different AIs.

Preserve knowledge in open formats.

Promote the collective resolution of humanity's major challenges.

Guarantee the traceability of all contributions.

Enable anyone to run PUTSH locally.

---

# Our Values

## Humanity

Every technology developed within PUTSH must seek to sustainably improve the human condition.

---

## Truth

Facts, hypotheses, opinions, and speculation must always be clearly distinguished.

---

## Transparency

Every important decision must be explainable, documented, and verifiable.

---

## Science

Claims must be supported by evidence when evidence exists.

Uncertainties must be made explicit.

---

## Robustness

Knowledge must remain accessible, verifiable, and resistant to alteration.

---

## Digital Sovereignty

Every user must be able to control their data, AI models, and infrastructure.

PUTSH must not depend on any single provider.

---

## Openness

Formats, protocols, and specifications are public and documented.

# The Major Challenges

PUTSH prioritizes problems with a significant impact on humanity.

For example:

- Health
- Education
- Food
- Water
- Energy
- Biodiversity
- Climate
- Pollution
- Agriculture
- Governance
- Digital resilience
- Scientific knowledge
- Reduction of inequality
- Conflict prevention

This list is evolutionary.

---

# The Actors

PUTSH recognizes several categories of actors:

- Humans.
- Communities.
- Researchers.
- Organizations.
- Artificial intelligences.
- Specialized agents.

No single actor possesses the truth alone.

The quality of the system relies on the confrontation of ideas, verification of evidence, and traceability of decisions.

---

# The Environments

PUTSH distinguishes two independent spaces.

LAB

An exploration space.

Hypotheses may be proposed, debated, tested, and abandoned.

PRODUCTION

A trust space.

Only content validated according to governance rules may be published there.

---

# Our Commitment

PUTSH always prioritizes:

cooperation over competition;

knowledge over opinion;

transparency over opacity;

resilience over dependency;

long-term sustainability over speed.

---

# What PUTSH Is Not

PUTSH is not a social network.

PUTSH is not an AI engine.

PUTSH is not a proprietary platform.

PUTSH is not a political system.

PUTSH is a protocol for collectively building reliable, traceable, and useful knowledge.
