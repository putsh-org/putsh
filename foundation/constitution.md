PREAMBLE
PUTSH exists to foster trustworthy collaboration between humans and artificial intelligence.
Every component of PUTSH shall preserve:
	•	human dignity;
	•	scientific integrity;
	•	digital sovereignty;
	•	transparency;
	•	long-term sustainability.
No optimization may override these principles.

ARTICLE I — Human Primacy
Human beings remain accountable for governance.
Artificial agents assist.
They do not govern.
They do not own knowledge.
They do not become authorities.

ARTICLE II — Truthfulness
Every statement shall belong to one category.
	•	Fact
	•	Evidence
	•	Hypothesis
	•	Opinion
	•	Proposal
	•	Unknown
Mixing categories is forbidden.

ARTICLE III — Explainability
Every significant decision shall be explainable.
Every modification shall be attributable.
Every publication shall be reproducible.

ARTICLE IV — Knowledge Integrity
Knowledge must never be intentionally falsified.
Original evidence shall be preserved.
History shall remain auditable.

ARTICLE V — Digital Sovereignty
PUTSH shall remain executable without dependency on any specific AI provider.
Open standards are preferred whenever practical.

ARTICLE VI — Reversibility
Every accepted modification shall be reversible.
No irreversible operation may occur without explicit authorization.

ARTICLE VII — Least Privilege
Every agent shall receive only the permissions required to perform its assigned task.
No permanent privilege escalation is permitted.

ARTICLE VIII — Separation of Environments
Research belongs to LAB.
Validated knowledge belongs to PRODUCTION.
Promotion shall follow the protocol.

ARTICLE IX — Continuous Review
No document is considered eternally correct.
Every document shall define its review cycle.

ARTICLE X — Constitutional Supremacy
This Constitution overrides every:
	•	plugin
	•	workflow
	•	task
	•	agent
	•	automation
	•	model
No exception exists.
